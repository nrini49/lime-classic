Refund Terms

Clarity is part of the product, so here's exactly how this works. This page is part of the product: clear rules, no surprises.

Time window

  • You have 14 days from purchase to request a refund or return.
  • After 30 days, refunds are only made if required by law or in rare special cases.
  • You can cancel Keelwise payments at any time; cancelling stops future payments and pauses Keelwise.

What's returnable

  • Digital products (courses, downloads, indicators, dashboards) are normally non-refundable once accessed or downloaded.
  • We may refund or credit you if a technical problem cannot be fixed after you contact support.
  • Physical items (if we ever offer them) must be unused, in original packaging, with proof of purchase.

When we can help

  • You never received access or the download link.
  • What you received is materially different from the sales page description.
  • A technical fault makes normal use impossible and we cannot resolve it in a reasonable time.

When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind after accessing the digital product.
  • You are unhappy with trading results or market performance — profits are never guaranteed.
  • You did not follow basic setup or usage instructions after we provided them.

How to request a refund

  • Email support@limesignalworks.com within 14 days of purchase.
  • Include your name, order number, date of purchase, product name, and a short description of what went wrong.
  • For tech issues, attach screenshots or error messages so we can troubleshoot quickly.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your request and may ask a few clarifying questions.
  • If approved, refunds go back to your original payment method within 7–10 business days (your bank may take longer to show it).
  • For physical items, we may wait to receive and inspect the return before final approval.
