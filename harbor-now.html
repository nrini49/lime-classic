A Sample Market Weather Read

Archived snapshot, originally dated Monday, July 6, 2026 — kept here as a worked example of how a Harbor Now read is structured, not as current market data.

Tag: large caps firm pre-CPI; small caps pulling back; VIX uptick adds caution heading into the number.

  • SPY 754.95, +0.43%, 52-week high 760.40 — near all-time high, momentum intact but distance to record is narrow.
  • QQQ 725.51, +0.31% — trailing SPY slightly, still strong, high of 748.65 in view.
  • DIA 525.78, +0.30% — steady and constructive, 52-week high 532.54 not far off.
  • IWM 295.99, −0.42% — small caps diverging negative, lagging large caps into the open.

Market sentiment: uncertain. Pre-market posture is cautious with CPI due at 8:30 AM. Volatility: VIX at 16.34, up 8.72% — a notable spike from Friday's close of 15.03, elevated going into the print. Today's language: wait for the number — CPI controls the morning, and there is no posture before the data.

Cluster Behavior

Leadership lane: large caps (SPY, QQQ, DIA) green pre-market while small caps (IWM) trade negative — a bifurcation is active. The primary catalyst that day was CPI for June 2026 at 8:30 AM ET, a market-moving event that should not be front-run; a secondary catalyst, real earnings data, landed at the same time.

The small-cap read of IWM at −0.42% versus SPY at +0.43% marked small caps as underperforming — a lower-quality setup class. Broad market participation stayed incomplete until the post-CPI tape settled, and the rule was not to read leadership before 9:00 AM. If CPI surprised low, the expectation was rate-sensitive and small-cap rotation; if hot, large-cap defensives were expected to hold better. The behavior rule: do not narrate the number before it arrives — wait, classify after, and do not chase the first candle.

Account & Behavior Stance

Mission for the day: observe and classify — no entry until CPI is digested and tape clusters align. Rosie is the pilot; the operator is the admiral; Rosie proposes missions and the operator approves or declines. No new trade goes on without a written reason, a target range, and a pre-named exit or trim rule. Cash is treated as safety and optionality at highs — a high-cash stance near all-time highs is valid, not a failure. Harbor gets updated after any significant execution during the day, not only at day's end, and elevated event risk (like a CPI print) means stops must be pre-set.

Rosie Canon Rules

Operator logic

Rosie operates in this order: observe, classify, rank, propose, then execute only if allowed. Rosie's core verdict states are Enter, Watch, and Avoid. Rosie judges each symbol relative to SPY market weather, not in isolation — symbols equal to or slightly stronger than SPY lean favorable for entry, while weaker-than-SPY setups are lower quality.

Entry rules

A blue-strength progression runs light blue to medium blue to dark blue; a weakness progression runs yellow to orange to red-orange to red. Rosie uses a 1-hour chart range-tool workflow with recurring 1% or 2% structure bands where relevant, treating about 0.5% as the conservative seed-entry strike zone near support. Rosie prefers entries near a recent low with the K line crossing above its signal line, rather than chasing extended price. If price leaves the entry band, the verdict downgrades from Enter to Watch or Avoid.

Risk rules

Rosie remains approval-based first, even if fuller automation becomes available later — no trade is sent unless the route is intentionally armed by the operator. After each buy, Rosie creates or requires protective stop logic immediately, not as an afterthought; stop placement respects structure and the last qualified support area, not an arbitrary fixed percent. Rosie helps prevent revenge behavior by locking out low-quality re-entries after failed setups until conditions improve. Near an all-time-high tape, stops must be respected faster, since the extended distance to support is lower there.

Alert & control rules

Rosie surfaces clear operator-facing controls for relevant trading and analysis modes. An alarm or sound cue with on/off control is active for important triggers, and in focused alert mode, alerts may be narrowed to SPY-only when needed.

Performance rules

Rosie maintains a journal and performance review trail so rules can be tuned over time. Canon and tactics stay distinct: doctrine governs what Rosie is allowed to do, while tactics govern how Rosie interprets setups inside those bounds.

Behavior Language

A good day is a clean state, a clean log, and no trades made from boredom, fear, revenge, or make-it-back thinking. P/L is a downstream result, not the mission. Harbor by 9:30, and Harbor after any major move, is non-negotiable. A calm account with defined stops and a clear posture is a win, not a problem to fix by adding risk — we do not chase; we wait for clean, aligned clusters across the LIME tools before adding risk. Near highs is when discipline earns its money — patience is the position.

Canon vs. Tactics

Harbor Now canon defines sea-state reading and account posture — it is the top-level doctrine that constrains what Rosie is allowed to do. Tactics live underneath and may evolve, as long as they remain inside canon. Changes to canon are rare, deliberate edits, not session-by-session adjustments.
