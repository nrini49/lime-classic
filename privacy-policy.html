Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026. LimeSignalWorks LLC ("LimeSignalWorks," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with our content online. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, mailing address.
  • Account and communication information: messages you send us, questions, feedback, subscription preferences.
  • Financial-related information you choose to share: investment objectives, time horizon, risk tolerance, and other self-reported financial details (we do not collect credit card numbers on this site unless clearly stated at checkout through a secure processor).
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages visited, time and date of visit, and referring URL.
  • Cookie and tracking data: information collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to understand how you use our website and to improve it.

We do not intentionally collect information from children under 13, and our services are directed to adults.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website, content, and services.
  • To send market commentary, Harbor Brief updates, and other emails you subscribe to.
  • To respond to your inquiries, requests, and customer support needs.
  • To personalize content, including displaying information that may be more relevant to your interests.
  • To analyze website usage and performance, including through analytics tools.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms, policies, and rights.

We may aggregate or de-identify information so it can no longer reasonably identify you and use that data for analysis, research, and improvement.

Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Understand how visitors use our site.
  • Improve performance and security.

You can usually set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you disable cookies, some features of the website may not function properly.

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or similar tools) that collect information about your use of the site over time and across different pages.

Legal Basis and Privacy Rights (Including California/Ohio Visitors)

Depending on where you live, you may have certain privacy rights under laws such as the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and proposed or future Ohio privacy legislation. Subject to applicable law, these may include the right to:

  • Know what personal data we collect and how we use it.
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Opt out of certain targeted advertising or the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information, where applicable.

To exercise these rights or submit a privacy request, contact us using the details in the "Contact us" section below.

At this time, we do not sell your personal information as that term is defined under California or similar state laws.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following situations:

  • Service providers: With trusted third parties who perform services for us, such as website hosting, email delivery, analytics, payment processing, or customer support. They are only permitted to use your information to provide services to us and must protect it.

  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or other corporate transaction involving LimeSignalWorks, where permitted by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
