The Official Moneytree Manual

A clean, grandma-ready user manual for the top, mid, and lower pane indicators — what each one is for, how and when to use it, and a short tip to keep it honest in practice.

Top Pane Indicators

Lime Color Weather™ — Market Weather Bands

Goal: Color Weather shows the current market “weather” so we know whether conditions favor buying, holding, or staying small. How and when: keep it on at all times and check it before any entry, exit, or position size decision so your actions match the current regime. Tip: trade boldly only in friendly weather; in storms, use your smallest size.

Lime 8 Filter Stack™ — Safety Gate

Goal: Filter Stack combines several safety checks into a single pass/fail view so we do not enter shaky trades. How and when: look at it right before opening or adding to any position; act only when the stack is mostly supportive, and stand aside when it is mixed or negative. Tip: mixed signals mean instant wait mode — no trade beats a forced trade.

Lime 5 StopWatcher I™ — First Stop

Goal: StopWatcher I gives a clear, rule-based first stop level so risk is defined before the trade begins. How and when: use it at entry to place your protective stop exactly where the model suggests, and check that the dollar risk matches your account rules. Tip: set the stop, then honor it — never widen stops after entry.

Lime 6 StopWatcher II™ — Trailing Guard

Goal: StopWatcher II provides a disciplined trailing stop path that locks in progress as price moves in our favor. How and when: switch focus to it after a trade has gained enough; move your stop along its guidance instead of guessing when to protect profits. Tip: let winners pay for future tries — trail stops, don't chase every tick.

Lime 7 StopWatcher III™ — Harvest Line

Goal: StopWatcher III highlights zones where a mature move is likely ending so we can harvest or tighten risk. How and when: watch it when a trade has run well or feels stretched; use it to decide where to take profits or clamp down your stop aggressively. Tip: grateful exits beat perfect exits — trim size when moves feel extreme.

Date and Price Range Tool (Top Pane) — Swing Ruler

Goal: the range tool measures how far and how long a chosen swing has moved, giving exact percent and time. How and when: draw the box from swing start to swing end whenever you study a move, so you can compare it against other swings and set realistic targets. Tip: big moves usually need real time — tiny time plus huge move, be cautious.

Mid Pane Indicators

Date and Price Range (Mid Pane) — Wave Ruler

Goal: gives a clean measure of the exhaustion wave being studied (how far, how long). How and when: wrap the exhaustion segment with the box so you can see “this last push was X% over Y days” and judge fatigue. Tip: tired waves often rhyme in size — shorter waves after giants signal caution.

Lime Color Weather™ (mid) — Trunk Weather

Goal: here, Color Weather tells us whether exhaustion waves are forming with or against the larger trend. How and when: always keep it visible; treat tired readings in up-weather differently than tired readings in down-weather. Tip: tired in uptrends favors gentle trims; tired in downtrends favors full exits.

Lime 3 Ark™ — Position Ark

Goal: Ark lays out a safe position plan for the current move — how big to be and how to carry it. How and when: consult Ark before committing size; use its guidance to choose initial size, add-zones, and when the trip is probably complete. Tip: let the Ark cap your ambition — size first, story second, always.

Lime 9 Exhaustion™ — Tiredness Gauge

Goal: Exhaustion shows whether a move is fresh, mid-journey, or worn out, so we avoid buying the last gasp. How and when: check it before entering and before taking profits; favor entries when readings are refreshed and respect exits when exhaustion is high. Tip: respect tired signals over gut feelings — fresh energy beats late chasing, always.

Lime 12 MaxSize Grid™ — Size Limits

Goal: MaxSize Grid defines the largest allowed position for each situation so no single idea can threaten the account. How and when: use whenever planning a trade; look up the symbol's current row and never exceed that size, even when a trade feels “certain.” Tip: hard limits protect you from yourself — big conviction, same max size rules.

Lower Pane Indicators

Lime Color Weather™ (lower) — Roots Weather

Goal: in the lower pane, Color Weather keeps the same regime story under all watchlists and rotation tools. How and when: leave it on; read leadership and rotation differently in good weather versus storms, and adjust aggressiveness accordingly. Tip: strong roots need friendly soil conditions — in bad soil, prune risk ruthlessly.

Lime Watch Session — Session Rhythm

Goal: Watch Session shows which part of the trading day or week we are in and how that slice usually behaves. How and when: use intraday or across days to decide when to be most active, when to fade noise, and when to stand aside. Tip: trade your best hours, skip junk hours — quiet windows deserve smaller expectations.

Lime 2 Navigation Sensor Array™ — Radar Board

Goal: Nav Sensor Array gives a cockpit-style radar of key indexes, sectors, or assets so we see where force is coming from. How and when: use at the start of each session and before any new trade to see which side of the market is in control. Tip: fly with the wind, not against it — ignore outliers when radar mostly agrees.

Lime 4 Rebounder™ — Bounce Hunter

Goal: Rebounder highlights zones where sharp selloffs or runs are likely to bounce, offering controlled counter-moves. How and when: use after volatility spikes or pullbacks; stalk rebounds only when Rebounder says the “floor” is likely nearby and the weather is not hostile. Tip: bounce trades demand smaller size always — skip rebounds during outright storm regimes.

Lime 10 Rotation Ballroom™ — Dance Floor Map

Goal: Rotation Ballroom shows how money is rotating among groups so we can move with the flow instead of guessing. How and when: use for swing and position trading; move capital toward the leaders entering the ballroom and away from tired dancers. Tip: rotate patiently, not every single day — favor fresh leaders over exhausted stars.

Lime 11a Sector-Index Leads™ — Leaderboard

Goal: Sector-Index Leads ranks sectors and indexes so we immediately know who is leading and who is lagging. How and when: use in the daily review; prefer trades aligned with the current leaders and avoid chronic laggards unless bottom-fishing by rule. Tip: leaders deserve first place in watchlists — laggards are guilty until proven strong.

Lime 11b Rank+Vote Ballroom™ — Consensus Score

Goal: Rank+Vote combines several models into one conviction score, so you see a simple “who wins” number. How and when: use as the final tie-breaker when choosing among candidates; the highest rank+vote gets attention first. Tip: let the votes break stubborn ties — top scores get attention, not guarantees.
